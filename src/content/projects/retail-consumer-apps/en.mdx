## Context

Web and mobile applications for clients in the consumer and retail sectors — including Burger King, Popeyes Louisiana Chicken, and C&A — focused on consistent interfaces, reusable components, and responsive experiences across devices.

## My role

As a Frontend Engineer, I developed and maintained web and mobile applications, built reusable components, integrated REST APIs, and collaborated with designers and backend engineers throughout the agile delivery cycle.

## Key challenges

- Delivering digital experiences for **consumer and retail brands** with strong requirements for visual consistency and development efficiency.
- Ensuring **responsive layouts** and solid performance on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- Maintaining quality and delivery pace with **TDD**, defect fixes, and cross-functional collaboration.

## Solutions implemented

- Developed and maintained applications with **React.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS**.
- Built **reusable components** and integrated REST APIs via **Axios** and the **Fetch API**.
- Implemented **responsive layouts** for desktop, tablet, and mobile experiences.
- Optimized **frontend performance**, fixed defects, and participated in agile ceremonies, technical refinements, and **TDD** practices.

## Results

- Greater interface consistency and development efficiency through reusable component architecture.
- Responsive experiences aligned with retail and consumer brand needs.
- More predictable deliveries through continuous collaboration across design, frontend, and backend.
