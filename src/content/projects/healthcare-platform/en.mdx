## Context

Healthcare web product with complex business rules, running continuously with users who depend on it for critical day-to-day workflows. I worked as a consultant, in parallel with my main role, to support and evolve the application.

## My role

Front-end Developer responsible for supporting and evolving features, focused on stability, regression prevention and continuous improvement of the user experience.

## Key challenges

- Keeping **critical features stable** in production while delivering new demands.
- Handling **complex business rules** reflected in long forms, dynamic validations and flows with many states.
- Diagnosing and fixing **production bugs** without introducing new issues.

## Solutions implemented

- Developed and maintained interfaces and flows with **Vue.js, JavaScript, HTML and CSS**.
- Fixed production bugs through **systematic debugging**: technical analysis, scenario reproduction and validation before delivery.
- Improved **forms, validations, modals and API integrations**, reducing inconsistencies and preventing regressions.
- Actively participated in **code reviews and pair programming** to raise the team's code quality.
- Collaborated on **technical refinement** of tasks, improving delivery predictability.

## Results

- Critical features remained stable throughout the entire engagement, with production bugs handled quickly and safely.
- More consistent validations and flows, reducing rework and support tickets.
- A more mature refinement process, with more predictable deliveries for the team.
