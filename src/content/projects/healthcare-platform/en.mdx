## Context

Healthcare web platform with complex business rules, running continuously with users who depend on it for critical scheduling workflows and day-to-day functionality. I worked as a consultant to support and evolve the application in production.

## My role

Frontend Software Engineer (consultant) responsible for maintaining and evolving the platform, sustaining legacy code, refining interfaces, and reducing regression risk in a domain sensitive to operational stability.

## Key challenges

- Sustaining **critical scheduling workflows** and production functionality with complex business rules.
- Evolving forms, validations, and integrations **without breaking legacy application behavior**.
- Diagnosing and fixing **production issues** systematically, reducing operational inconsistencies.

## Solutions implemented

- Maintained and evolved the platform with **Vue.js, JavaScript, TypeScript, HTML, and CSS**, including professional experience with **Angular** in the product ecosystem.
- Developed and refined **forms, validation rules, modals, responsive interfaces**, and **REST API** integrations.
- Sustained **legacy code** while preserving compatibility with existing application behavior.
- Resolved production issues through **systematic debugging**, contributing via code reviews, pair programming, and technical refinement.

## Results

- Reduced regression risk and fewer operational inconsistencies in critical flows.
- Stronger code quality and delivery predictability through code review, pair programming, and technical refinement.
- Platform supported and evolved without compromising compatibility with established behavior.
