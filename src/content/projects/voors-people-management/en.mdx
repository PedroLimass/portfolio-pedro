## Context

Voors is a people management platform by VIASOFT, used by companies to organize HR processes. The product required modern, responsive and efficient interfaces aligned with a consistent visual standard.

## My role

Front-end Developer responsible for turning UI/UX designs into functional, reusable components, integrating them with application data and ensuring performance and compatibility.

## Key challenges

- Translating **UI/UX layouts into components faithful to the design** while keeping them reusable and maintainable.
- Ensuring **responsiveness and cross-browser compatibility** across the platform.
- Maintaining **performance** even on data-rich, interactive screens.

## Solutions implemented

- Built responsive interfaces with **React and Tailwind CSS**, following the product's visual standard.
- Converted layouts into **functional, reusable components**, reducing code duplication.
- Integrated with **REST APIs** to consume and manipulate application data.
- Optimized **performance and user experience** on the main flows.
- Supported **process mapping and documentation**, proposing improvements to internal and operational flows.

## Results

- Interfaces consistent with the design and responsive on desktop, tablet and mobile.
- A reusable component base that sped up the development of new screens.
- Better documented internal flows, with operational improvements proposed and adopted by the team.
